## Ideal Technosignatures
Technosignatures should be narrowband with a constant drift rate across long observations. Wide-band signals and inconsistent drift rates are not characteristic of technosignatures and are usually attributed to radio frequency interference (RFI). The first image is an artificial technosignature used for testing and the second is a technosignature detected from the Voyager 1 telescope.
